# Weekly competitor digest — 70 channels

**The set.** 70 channels in the AI/automation niche, 3,064,260 subscribers
between them. Median channel is 30,800 subs, so the total is carried by a
handful of large accounts.

**Publishing.** Median pace across the set is under 10 videos a month. Six
channels publish 30 or more. Those six average 1,933 subs/month of growth
against 972 for the 41 channels at 10 or fewer — a 2.0x gap in favour of volume.

**Gone quiet.** 14 channels have not published in 90 days or more. That is 20%
of the set idle within a single quarter, which is the churn rate this niche runs
at.

**Fastest growing.** Jack Vs. AI at 5,607 subs/month, Ed Hill at 5,365, Andy Lo
at 4,273. All three publish under 10 videos a month.

**What to watch.** The volume gap and the top of the ranking disagree. Worth a
closer look before drawing a lesson from either.
