# Dormancy report

**14 of 70 channels are dormant** — no upload in 90+ days. At 20% of the set per
quarter, this niche has a brutal attrition rate.

**Faceless is where it concentrates.** Only 7 of the 70 channels are fully
faceless, and 5 of those 7 are among the dormant. That is a 71% failure rate for
the faceless format against roughly 14% for the rest of the set.

**Read.** Faceless production is the cheapest format to start and the hardest to
sustain. The operator burns out because there is no personal audience pulling
them back. Expect a faceless channel to need 6-9 months of unrewarded output
before compounding starts, which is longer than most operators budget for.

**Recommendation.** If going faceless, commit to a fixed cadence you can hold
for at least 40 videos. The channels that survived all did.
