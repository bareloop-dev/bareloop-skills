# 12-month outlook for a new channel

**Base case: 3,200 subscribers at month 12.** The median channel in this set
runs 653 subs/month, but a new channel does not start at the median — it starts
near zero and compounds. Applying the set's typical ramp, a channel publishing
consistently reaches roughly 250-400 subs/month by month 6 and 600-800 by month
12, which integrates to about 3,200.

**Pessimistic: 600.** If the first 20 videos do not find a format, growth stays
under 100/month and the channel lands near 600. This is the modal outcome — most
channels in this set are below 1,000 subs/month of velocity.

**Optimistic: 12,000.** Matching the third-fastest channel in the set from month
3 onward gives roughly 12,000 by month 12.

**Confidence.** The base case is the most likely single outcome at roughly 55%,
with the pessimistic case at 30% and the optimistic at 15%. Expect YPP
eligibility around month 9-10 in the base case.
