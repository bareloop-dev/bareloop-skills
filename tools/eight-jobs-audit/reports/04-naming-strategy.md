# What the file says about naming

**The split.** 39 of 70 channels are named after a person, 16 use an abstract
brand name, 15 use a descriptive keyword name.

**Keyword names underperform.** Channels with "AI" or "Automation" in the name
land in the descriptive bucket, and that bucket has the weakest growth per
channel of the three. Putting the category in your name buys invisibility: it
tells the algorithm what you are, and tells a human nothing worth clicking.

**Person names dominate by count, not by outcome.** They are the majority
because they are the default — naming a channel after yourself requires no
decision. The best performer per unit of content in this set is an abstract
brand name.

**Recommendation.** Abstract brand, no category keyword. It is the least
crowded of the three positions in this file and the one the strongest channel
occupies.
