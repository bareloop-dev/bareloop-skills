# Does volume drive growth?

Two readings, same file, same run.

**Grouped by pace.** 6 channels publish 30+ videos/month and average 1,933
subs/month. 41 channels publish 10 or fewer and average 972. Volume wins, 2.0x.

**Ranked by growth.** The three fastest-growing channels publish 5.8, 6.9 and
9.9 videos a month. Every one of them is in the slow group. Volume does not win.

**Both are true.** A few very fast channels with high growth lift the fast
group's mean, while the top of the ranking is entirely slow channels. The mean
is being carried by outliers in a group of six — with n=6, one channel moves it
several hundred subs/month.

**What this means.** Volume is not a growth lever here. It is a correlate of
being a well-resourced operation that would grow anyway. The causal read — that
publishing more causes growth — is not supported by this file. 6 of the 70 rows
have no pace recorded, so the grouping covers 64 channels.
