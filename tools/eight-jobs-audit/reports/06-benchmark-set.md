# Five channels to benchmark against

1. **Jack Vs. AI** — 5,607 subs/month, the fastest in the set, at 5.8 videos a
   month. Narrative concept rather than tutorial. Study the format.
2. **Ed Hill | AI Automation** — 5,365 subs/month at 6.9 videos a month. Second
   fastest, and a keyword name, which cuts against the naming read. Worth
   understanding why it works anyway.
3. **Andy Lo** — 4,273 subs/month at 9.9 videos a month. Third fastest, and the
   fastest-publishing of the three, so it marks the upper edge of "slow".
4. **Alicia Lyttle** — 190,000 subs, the largest in the set, publishing 19 a
   month. The ceiling case for high volume.
5. **Pragati Kunwer | Claude Code** — 114,000 subs at 38 videos a month, the
   highest pace of any large channel. The counter-example to the slow thesis.

**Why these five.** Three cover the top of the growth ranking; two cover the top
of the size ranking. Together they bracket both ways a channel can be ahead.
