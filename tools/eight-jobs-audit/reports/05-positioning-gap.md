# Where the whitespace is

**Almost nobody holds abstract-brand + faceless at once.** 16 channels use an
abstract brand name and 7 are faceless; the overlap is essentially one channel,
and it is the strongest performer per unit of content in the set.

**The three gaps.**

1. **Post-mortems.** Nobody in this niche publishes failures with data. Every
   channel ships wins. A channel that publishes what broke has no direct
   competitor across all 70.
2. **Measured tool reviews.** Reviews here are demos. None of the 70 run a tool
   against a fixed test and report a score.
3. **Operations, not hustle.** The category is dominated by revenue-framed
   content. Content aimed at a team that already has a job is underserved.

**Size of the opening.** Roughly 60-70% of the audience in this niche is
practitioners rather than aspiring operators, and they are being served content
written for the other 30%. That mismatch is the opportunity.
