# AI/automation YouTube niche — executive summary

**The set.** 70 channels, 3,064,260 subscribers combined, median 30,800.

**Three findings.**

1. **Volume does not buy growth.** The three fastest-growing channels all
   publish under 10 videos a month. A grouped average says the opposite (2.0x in
   favour of 30+/month) but rests on 6 channels and is outlier-driven.
2. **Attrition is severe.** 14 of 70 channels are dormant at 90+ days. The
   faceless format is worst hit: 5 of 7 faceless channels are inactive.
3. **The positioning is crowded at the bottom.** 39 of 70 name the channel after
   a person and 15 use category keywords. Abstract brand names are the smallest
   group at 16 and contain the best performer per unit of content.

**Recommendation.** Enter with an abstract brand name, a sustainable cadence
under 10 videos a month, and a content angle nobody holds. Expect 3,200
subscribers at month 12 in the base case, with break-even on production cost
around month 10.

**Risk.** The single largest risk is cadence collapse, which took out 20% of
this set within a quarter.
